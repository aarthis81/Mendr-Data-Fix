      How to play

      Cyan dodecahedrons: repairable defects

      Fixable cells and risky code patterns. Fly close and press Space to clean them for +100 × combo.

      Yellow hexagonal guards: your validation rules

      Built from safe rules in your code. Pulse them for +150 × combo and +5% integrity.

      Crimson octahedrons: protected IDs and outliers

      Never pulse these. It costs 25% integrity and resets your combo. Dash clear when their shockwave ring expands (15% if it hits).

      Winning: clear all cyan defects across 3 waves for a +500 bonus. Multi-repairs in one pulse stack your combo up to 6×. At 0% integrity the run ends.

      Controls and strategy

      Tip: wait until two or three cyan defects drift away from crimson keys, then pulse once for a multi-repair and a bigger combo.
